# Shadow Sight

**First Referenced:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left.'

**Other References:**

Our Final Acts

Shadow Sight is an enhanced ability triggered in humans that have been mentally and physically tortured and modified by the [Shadow Entities](/entities/shadow-entity). All members of [The Black-eyed Army](/people/the-black-eyed-army), as well as [Jonathan Cortin](/people/jonathan-cortin) and [Ward Shaw](/people/ward-shaw-the-sword), the leader of [The Black-eyed Army](/people/the-black-eyed-army) possess this ability.
In addition to providing a means to see within [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink), Shadow Sight also allows an individual to perceive various multidimensional entities, such as the [Shadow Entities](/entities/shadow-entity) themselves, as well as the true shape of the [Soul Reader (Book)](/devices/soul-reader-book). It also provides the ability to more closely analyse normal physical entities such as humans, which can be used to identify whether someone is lying or telling the truth.
