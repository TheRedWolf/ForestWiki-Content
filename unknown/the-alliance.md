# The Alliance

**First Referenced:**

The Moon Aflame (Part 30

**Other References:**

'I think I may have somehow missed the end of the world...'

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left.'

The Alliance is an agreement struck between [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) and the [Brownshirts](/races/brownshirt), approximately 1000 years ago. As part of this alliance, the [Brownshirts](/races/brownshirt) assisted [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) in building both the [Inner Shield](/devices/inner-shield) and [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield), and growing the [Branch World](/worlds/branch-world)s that gave the human section of the multiverse proper structure between the shields.

It is currently unknown whether further species or entities are members of the Alliance.
