# Diamond Farmer

**First Referenced:**

The New Exodus Vanguard (Part 3)

Diamond Farmers are large, crystalline, jellyfish-like entities. They are associated with the [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony) and are so named due to their structure largely comprising of diamond. From observations, the farmers appear to tend large emerald fields, and also contain tentacles capable of generating electricity through piezoelectric actions.

Previously, diamond farmers had been attacked by people associated with [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world), in an attempt to mine them as a resource. The result of this negligent greed along with other actions has resulted in ongoing hostility between humans and the [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony).

**Traits:**

Crystalline, capable of flight in a fashion similar to the [Ruby Cubes](/entities/ruby-cube). Able to generate significant electrical charge through piezoelectric action of the tentacles.
