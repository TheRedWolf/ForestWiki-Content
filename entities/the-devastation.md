# The Devastation

**First Referenced:**

The Fountain of Youth

**Other References:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left. (Part 6)', 'The New Exodus Vanguard'

The Devastation is a gargantuan entity, massive even in comparison to the [Multiverse Titan](/entities/multiverse-titan)s, which themselves are the size of several universe bubbles. Described by [The Sword](/people/ward-shaw-the-sword) as being 'As big as you can imagine... and then a thousand times bigger than that.'

The Devastation was responsible for [The Crushing Fist](/unknown/the-crushing-fist). The edge of one its flailings grazed against the [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield) created by [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) empire, severely weakening the shield and destroying many realities.

In addition, the flailings of The Devastation also killed [Her Glory](/entities/her-glory), and scattered her realities across the multiverse.

**Traits:**

Destructive, capable of wiping out multiple universes through its random, wild flailing. Does not seem to be aware of what it is doing.
