# Dreamer On High

**First Referenced:**

The Moon Aflame

**Traits:**

An ancient and powerful entity, capable of affecting the mental state of a large number of people through visual stimulation. Mistaken for one of two moons in orbit around one version of Earth and awoken by the impact of a near light-speed object, the Dreamer attempted to build a device incorporating multiple members of the [Brownshirt](/races/brownshirt) community in order to provide an escape path for itself and a large number of human worshippers/followers from [The Crushing Fist](/unknown/the-crushing-fist).

Currently deceased due to multiple impacts by near light-speed high mass kinetic weapons. Initial appraisal has identified what appears to be large quantities of brain tissue.

Potentially an entity related to the [Regret Demon](/entities/regret-demon), or the [Multiverse Titan](/entities/multiverse-titan)'s [Regeneration Core](/entities/regeneration-core) encountered in The Fountain of Youth series.
