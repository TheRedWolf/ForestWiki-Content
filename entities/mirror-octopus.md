# Mirror Octopus

**First Referenced:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left. (Part 1)'

**Additional References:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left. (Part 3)'

Described as a titanic, octopus-like creature with highly reflective skin. Originally encountered by [Higgins](/people/higgins), [Cristina Thompson](/people/cristina-thompson) and [Vasiliev Blaku](/people/vasiliev-blaku) when [The Sword](/people/ward-shaw-the-sword) commenced his engagement against [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink), the Mirror Octopus killed or wounded a large number of troops by simply hitting or crushing them with its tentacles.

After a method for its capture and termination was discovered by [Cristina Thompson](/people/cristina-thompson), it was dragged from [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink) by a fleet of large trucks. Its corpse did not decompose, suggesting it may not be affected by traditional micro-organisms responsible for biological decay. It remains a deceased landmark near [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink).
