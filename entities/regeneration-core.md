# Regeneration Core

**First Referenced:**

The Fountain of Youth

**Other References:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left. (Part 6)'

The Regeneration Core is a remnant of a once huge titan of an entity which existed outside of any given universe. While originally born within a universe, over time it grew and was forced to exist within the multiverse, outside the 'bubbles' of the universe it once called home.

After its defeat and destruction due to a battle with an even larger entity, the Titan sent a compressed seed of memory, energy and material, the Regeneration Core, nineteen and a half billion years into the future, where [Ward Shaw (The Sword)](/people/ward-shaw-the-sword) existed.
