# Inner Shield

**First Referenced:**

The Desolate Guardians (Part 5)

**Additional References:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left.'

The Inner Shield is described as a large, golden shield that protects [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) and prevents unwanted inter-dimensional ingress from outside. The Inner Shield is a last line of defense, situated behind a barrier made of [Farming Worlds](/worlds/farming-world) and other realities, beyond which is the [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield), which is intended to protect all of the human populated realities in that section of the multiverse.

The Inner Shield is generated and supported by vast machines located on [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) and some supporting [Branch Worlds](/worlds/branch-world). Due to the immense compression the universes are under, the machinery is generating vast quantities of heat, which are affecting weather conditions across the entirety of [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world).

Due to the structures of both the Inner Shield, [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield) and [Branch Worlds](/worlds/branch-world), should the [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield) fail completely and the full compression of [The Crushing Fist](/unknown/the-crushing-fist) be exerted on the Inner Shield, [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) will be the focal point of the destruction.

The [Inner Shield](/devices/inner-shield), [Outer Shield](/devices/outer-shield) and [Branch Worlds](/worlds/branch-world) were constructed with the assistance of the [Brownshirts](/races/brownshirt), who helped [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) when it was a new power in the multiverse.
