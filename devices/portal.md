# Portal

Portal could refer to the following:

[Portal (Brownshirt)](/devices/portal-brownshirt)

The type of portal generated by [Brownshirts](/races/brownshirt), which form a slightly blurry hole in space.

[Techno-Portal](/devices/techno-portal)

Generated by technology such as that found in the various [Portal hub facility](/devices/portal-hub-facility) installations in the multiverse. Originally created by [Her Glory](/entities/her-glory) when seeking a cure to her husband's condition.

[Shadow Sphere](/unknown/shadow-sphere)

A type of dark portal to highly dangerous universes such as those of the [Preacher](/entities/preacher). Related to the [Hunger Shadows](/entities/shadow-entity).
