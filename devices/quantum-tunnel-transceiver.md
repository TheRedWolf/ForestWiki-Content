# Quantum Tunnel Transceiver

**First referenced:**

The Desolate Guardians (Part 2)

The Quantum Tunnel Transceiver is a device used to connect to and communicate with multiple internet and networking systems on parallel Earths in the multiverse structure. Accessed via the network node/hub on the destroyed Earth inhabited by [Heath](/people/heath), an IT systems administrator. Used for bidirectional communication with [Cristina Thompson](/people/cristina-thompson) first encountered in The Portal in the Forest, as well as [Noah Fulmer](/people/noah-fulmer) and many others across the multiverse.

The network node requires a direct neural interface and virtual world environment to properly interact with all of the connected systems, which also contains a long term life support system.
