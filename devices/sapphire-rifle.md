# Sapphire Rifle

**First Referenced:**

Our Final Acts (Part 6)

**Additional References:**

Our Final Acts (Part 8)

The Sapphire Rifle is a weapon based on or derived from the [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony) and hidden in a secret cache by [Cristina Thompson](/people/cristina-thompson). Many duplicates of the original single weapon possessed by Cristina were created along with multiple duplicates of [Noah Fulmer](/people/noah-fulmer) using the [Quantum Duplication Rift/cleft](/devices/quantum-duplication-rift-cleft) present in Noah's home universe.

Like many weapons based on [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony) technology, the Sapphire rifle is capable of dealing enormous destructive power. When fired, the rifle launches a deep blue dodecahedron projectile which explodes on contact with solid matter. The Sapphire rifle itself was created by humanity, rather than by the entities comprising the [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony), which explains its suitability for use by humans or [Yngtak](/races/yngtak).

When dropped by [Conn Thompson](/people/conn-thompson) after suffering a malfunction, a [Ruby Cube](/entities/ruby-cube) was able to detect and retrieve the weapon through unknown means.

**Traits:**
Original technology acquired from the [Gemstone Hegemony](/unknown/gemstone-hegemony), likely through theft or violence. Smooth with similar dimensions to a traditional assault rifle. Capable of causing significant destruction.
