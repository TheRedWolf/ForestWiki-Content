# Clint Alvarez

**First Referenced:**

The New Exodus Vanguard (Part 1)

**Additional References:**

The New Exodus Vanguard (Multiple Parts)

Clint Alvarez is a member of [The New Exodus Vanguard](/people/the-new-exodus-vanguard), and a member of [Kendrick Merrill](/people/kendrick-merrill)'s squad. After first attempting to sacrifice [Cheng Yao](/people/cheng-yao) to the [Closet Door Rift](/entities/closet-door-rift), and later admitting to both [Kendrick Merrill](/people/kendrick-merrill) and [Cheng Yao](/people/cheng-yao) that he had encountered the [Closet Door Rift](/entities/closet-door-rift) previously, he left the squad under threat of death should he return. Despite being separated from the squad, he was able to track them, encountering [Jennifer Pixley](/people/jennifer-pixley) in a frozen forest, although he still was not permitted to rejoin the squad.

Clint later encountered [Mona Wygant](/people/mona-wygant) and [Edgar Brace](/people/edgar-brace), revealing his unusual ability to find the other members of the squad despite the significant distance between them all. As Clint had been described as "The Finder" by a grey rider, [Mona Wygant](/people/mona-wygant) opted to allow him to stick with them in order to locate the missing squad members.

After locating [Kendrick Merrill](/people/kendrick-merrill), Clint was unsure whether he would be allowed to rejoin the squad. [Kendrick Merrill](/people/kendrick-merrill)'s decision to reform the squad and not lose any other members was a primary factor in Clint rejoining the squad, as his tracking ability would be necessary to locate the remaining members of the squad.

**Traits:**
Known to do whatever necessary to survive, including sacrificing those closest to him. Has the ability to detect or find people, even across vast distances and numerous realities.
