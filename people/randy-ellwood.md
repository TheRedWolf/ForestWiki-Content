# Randy Ellwood

**First Referenced:**

The New Exodus Vanguard (Part 1)

Randy Ellwood is described as being short and red-headed with a loud voice. She has a background in mechanics and a fascination with engines, as evidenced by her immediate partial disassembly and inspection of her First World designed and constructed motorbike, assigned to her as part of her kit in [The New Exodus Vanguard](/people/the-new-exodus-vanguard).

Randy recognized the discomfort [Kendrick Merrill](/people/kendrick-merrill) experienced when [Jennifer Pixley](/people/jennifer-pixley) made advances towards him, and sought to defuse them.

**Traits:**

Observant and mechanically minded, and displays some tom-boyish tendencies.
