# Earth 44

**First Referenced:**

'I was told that everyone I'd served with in the military died shortly after I left. (Part 4)'

Earth 44 is a desert variant of Earth, where [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink) was first encountered. Earth 44 was being used by [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) as a source of resources, resulting in ongoing conflict with "Rebels", likely inhabitants of that specific reality.

How exactly [The Ink](/unknown/the-ink) and the [Shadow Sphere](/unknown/shadow-sphere) ended up on Earth 44 is currently unknown.
