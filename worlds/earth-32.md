# Earth 32

**First Referenced:**

Our Final Acts [Part 1]

Similar to the [Farming World](/worlds/farming-world)s, various neighboring realities are, in part, dedicated to specific tasks. In the case of Earth 32, this task is waste processing as a planet-wide garbage dump. Earth 32 was briefly referenced by various refugees, suggesting the inhabitants of Earth 32 have also fled inwards toward [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world), away from the effects of [The Crushing Fist](/unknown/the-crushing-fist).
