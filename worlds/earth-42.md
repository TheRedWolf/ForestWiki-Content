# Earth 42

**First Referenced:**

Our Final Acts [Part 1]

Described in a brief conversation as being the version of Earth in which Beanie Babies are worth millions of dollars. Little else is known about Earth 42, other than refugees from it have also fled inward towards [The First World](/worlds/the-first-world) to escape [The Crushing Fist](/unknown/the-crushing-fist).
